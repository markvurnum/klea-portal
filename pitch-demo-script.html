FAQs

  • Can we change prices, services and add-ons? Yes. The whole catalogue, services, upsells, discounts, checklists and FAQs, is yours to change whenever you like.
  • Does it cover our area? The coverage check is one setting. We switch it to your Fylde Coast postcodes in a minute.
  • What does it cost to run? Hosting is around £5 to £10 a month, plus normal card fees and pennies per text. No per-seat software fees, no lock-in.
  • How quickly could we launch? About a week once payments and messaging are connected: we plug in Stripe, text and email sending, secure logins and your domain, then load your real team and prices.
  • Is our data ours? Yes. It runs on its own standalone database, shared with nothing and nobody else, and exportable at any time.
