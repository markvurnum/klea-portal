The tour

Ten parts of your platform

1

Clients book in two minutes

portal.kleahome.co.uk

Your services, your prices, your FAQs. Postcode check covers the Fylde Coast, so out-of-area enquiries are handled politely rather than wasting your time.

Add-ons are the ones you actually sell: oven clean, bed changes with a count, fridge and freezer. First visit is charged at the reset-clean rate, with your regular rate from visit two. Kleaners appear with photo, profile line and DBS badge, closest patch first.

2

Bookings arrive as requests, if you want

Bookings

A switch on the Bookings page decides whether website bookings confirm instantly or land as requests for you to approve. Nothing reaches a rota without your say-so when it is set to requests.

You can also add a booking yourself, with a custom amount that overrides the calculated price.

3

The rota, with time off built in

Rota

Everyone's week at a glance, or click a name to see just that Kleaner. Book holidays, sickness or any blocked-out day and it greys out on the rota.

Time off genuinely blocks work: nobody on leave is offered a new job from any source. If you book someone off over jobs they already have, you get told which ones need reassigning.

4

Your Guesty changeovers, automatically

Guesty

Connected to your account and syncing on its own every three hours. Every checkout becomes a changeover clean at 10:00, sized to finish by 15:00 before the next guest.

All of them land as requests for you to approve. Same-day turnarounds are flagged in red. If a guest cancels in Guesty, the clean cancels here, so nobody drives to an occupied property.

5

Your Kleaner's whole shift, on their phone

Kleaner app

Every Kleaner has their own login. One-tap call to the client, key and access notes, the full Klea Standard checklist, photo upload, Start and Finish.

They see their own jobs and rota, nothing else, and never what you charge. They can set their own working hours, log extra time and upload their DBS and documents themselves.

6

The Klea Standard, on every job

The checklist from your website is built into every clean: bedroom, bathroom, living room and kitchen, plus the deep clean extras. A domestic clean ticks the standard items, a deep clean ticks everything.

This is "every clean is photographed and rated" made operational. The standard never depends on memory.

7

Clients help themselves

My cleans

Clients move or cancel their own cleans, see the photos of every finished clean and leave the star rating.

Your policy is enforced by the software: free until midday the day before, 50% after. No awkward phone calls.

8

Staff files that watch themselves

Staff

A full employment file per Kleaner: right to work, DBS certificate and recheck date, training, contract, emergency contact, plus your company insurance and registrations.

Anything within 60 days of renewal turns amber and appears on your dashboard, so nothing lapses quietly.

9

Payroll and hiring

Payroll

Wages accrue from completed cleans, with a separate weekend rate where you set one. Click any Kleaner for a week-by-week breakdown of every job and the rate applied.

Your Become a Kleaner page feeds a hiring pipeline. One click turns an applicant into a staff file with every onboarding check flagged until done.

10

Your money, honestly

Reports

Stock levels for the Klea kit with reorder alerts, an expense ledger, and a monthly profit and loss built from real revenue, real wages and real spend.

Every action is recorded too, so you can always see who did what and when.

FAQs

  • Can we change prices, services and add-ons? Yes. The whole catalogue, services, add-ons, discounts, checklists and FAQs, is yours to change whenever you like.
  • Does it cover our area? Yes, it is set to the Fylde Coast and surrounding area. Out-of-area postcodes are declined politely at the booking stage.
  • Will our Kleaners see what we charge? No. Pricing is stripped out of everything a Kleaner can reach, not just hidden on screen.
  • What happens if a guest cancels in Guesty? The changeover cancels here automatically, so nobody travels to an occupied property.
  • What does it cost to run? Hosting is around £5 to £10 a month, plus normal card fees and pennies per text. No per-seat software fees, no lock-in.
  • How quickly could we go fully live? Around a week once payments and messaging are connected, and your own team and clients are loaded.
  • Is our data ours? Yes. It runs on its own database, shared with nothing and nobody else, and exportable at any time.
